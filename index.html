Modul Belajar 📚

Grammar dasar dari modul — Present, Past, Future, Articles, Prepositions, Pronouns, Singular-Plural, To be.

Present Tense

Positif: Subject + Verb 1 + Object
Negatif: Subject + do/does + not + Verb 1
Contoh: I learn English every day.

Past Tense

Positif: Subject + Verb 2 + Object
Contoh: We visited Grandma last Sunday. • go → went, eat → ate, see → saw

Future Tense

Rumus: Subject + will + Verb 1
Contoh: I will study tomorrow.

Articles: a / an / the

a + kata benda tunggal, an + huruf vokal, the + sudah jelas. Contoh: a book, an apple, the sun.

Prepositions

in, on, at, under, behind. Contoh: in the box, on the table, at school.

Pronouns & To be

I, you, he, she, we, they. To be: am, is, are. Contoh: She is happy. They are friends.

Tips Belajar 💡

🗣️ Bicara 5 menit setiap hari

Tidak perlu sempurna. Ceritakan kegiatanmu dalam Bahasa Inggris, walau hanya 5 menit.

🎧 Dengarkan, lalu tirukan

Dengarkan satu kalimat, jeda, lalu tirukan bunyinya.

📝 Tulis 3 kalimat baru

Setiap hari tulis 3 kalimat tentang harimu.

🔁 Ulangi dengan jarak

Ulangi kosakata hari ini, besok, dan 3 hari lagi.
